# Inspections and Offers

The field checklist covers VIN/title match, cold start, warning lights, OBD codes, fluids, leaks, transmission, brakes, steering, suspension, tires, rust, frame, HVAC, electronics, and road test. Each item can be `PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNKNOWN`, or `NOT_CHECKED`.

The offer calculator uses current asking price, conservative finished value, expected/high repairs, inspection, transport, transaction costs, target all-in ratio, and target margin. Recalculate after inspection findings change.
