# FAQ

## Does DealHound scrape Facebook?

No. The supported flow is copy/paste and screenshots supplied by the user.

## Are values guaranteed?

No. Values and repairs are planning-grade estimates requiring verification.

## What happens when a provider is down?

The listing remains available with degraded/manual evidence. Missing information explains what to do next.

## Who can see my listings?

All tenant-owned reads and writes are scoped by the authenticated account. Production verification must still include a cross-user browser test.
